# Integration asset provenance

Downloaded on 2 October 2026 from the brands' own public resource pages.
PNG copies are proportionally downsampled to 256px for web delivery. Shapes,
colors, proportions and transparency are preserved; no filters or effects apply
to the logos. Motion belongs to their surrounding containers and connection paths.

- Facebook: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo/
  Official Facebook Brand Asset Pack, `Logo/Primary Logo/Facebook_Logo_Primary.png`.
- Instagram: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/
  Official IG brand asset pack, `01 Static Glyph/01 Gradient Glyph/Instagram_Glyph_Gradient.png`.
- Buffer: https://buffer.com/press
  Official icon served at https://buffer.com/img/logos/buffer-logo.png.

The Meta company logo is intentionally not included. Its current guidance requires
approval: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/meta/company-brand/. The interface
identifies the connection descriptively as **Meta Direct**, without representing
it as a third social platform or suggesting partnership/endorsement.

Third-party trademarks belong to their respective owners. These assets identify
implemented connections and destinations; they are not part of Qurtiz's own mark.
